# 📊 AttritionIQ – Employee Attrition & Risk Analysis Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI** dashboard that analyzes employee attrition across **4,700 employees** and highlights the factors and employee groups most at risk of leaving, so HR teams can take action before talent is lost.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

Employee turnover is costly. This dashboard helps HR and business leaders to:

- Track the overall **attrition rate** and how it varies across the organization
- Identify **high-risk employee segments** (by department, role, tenure, age, etc.)
- Understand the **key drivers** behind attrition
- Support data-driven **retention decisions**
